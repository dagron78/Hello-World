# Delivery Specification

## Date-Bound Milestones

Baseline reference dates for this plan are **March 24, 2026**, **April 7, 2026**, and **May 26, 2026**. The milestone sequence below anchors to those dates and also maps successor-year equivalents for current planning continuity.

| Milestone | 2026 baseline target | Successor-year equivalent (2027) | Planning intent |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Internal scenario demo before next equivalent “proposed budget” work session | **March 24, 2026** | **March 23, 2027** | Deliver an internal demo package in advance of the next proposed-budget-style work session so scenario framing can be reviewed before public-facing preparation. |
| Tax-rate scenario pack readiness before hearing prep window | **April 7, 2026** | **April 6, 2027** | Complete and QA tax-rate scenarios ahead of hearing-prep activities to ensure hearing materials can be assembled without dependency risk. |
| Adoption support package before board adoption prep | **May 26, 2026** | **May 25, 2027** | Finalize board-adoption support artifacts in advance of adoption-prep workflows, including briefings, exhibits, and decision-support summaries. |

## Successor-Year Mapping Rule

For annual planning updates, use the same milestone ordering and shift each baseline to the nearest matching weekday in the successor year:

- March 24, 2026 (Tuesday) -> March 23, 2027 (Tuesday)
- April 7, 2026 (Tuesday) -> April 6, 2027 (Tuesday)
- May 26, 2026 (Tuesday) -> May 25, 2027 (Tuesday)

This maintains cadence relative to recurring governance and hearing-preparation cycles.
